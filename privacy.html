Privacy policy

Last updated 23 September 2026

Minutes is a personally run project for logging time and taking part in shared challenges. This notice explains what the app uses, where information goes and how to ask for help with your data.

Who to contact

The Minutes administrator maintains the app and handles privacy requests. For questions, access to your information, corrections or deletion requests, email minutes.app@outlook.com.

Information the app uses

  • Account information: your participant name, account identifier, roles and account-link or approval requests. Google sign-in supplies an identifier, email address and verification status, and can supply a display name and profile-photo URL.
  • Activity and challenges: dates, minutes, optional notes or activity labels, challenge details, memberships, round participation and results.
  • Existing participant records: historical minutes and participant identifiers can exist before Google linking. The administrator may reserve a known email address to connect an existing participant with their account.
  • Device and security information: saved preferences, cached app data and sign-in state. Hosting and security providers can also process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details and request or abuse-prevention signals.

Minutes does not request access to your Gmail messages or Google contacts, and does not receive your Google password.

How information is used

Information is used to recognise participants, link existing history, manage access and approvals, record minutes, calculate results, display shared activity, synchronise devices and maintain the service.

The app has no advertising integration or separate audience-analytics service. Security checks and providers' operational logging are different from audience analytics.

Shared activity, not a private journal

Participant names, challenge participation, logged minutes and notes are used in shared views. Administrators can access information needed to manage the app, accounts and approval requests.

The current version retains legacy data access during account migration. Activity records are not strictly isolated by challenge membership. Do not treat a challenge as a private group or put confidential or sensitive health information in names or notes.

Linking Google verifies your sign-in; it does not, by itself, make your activity history private.

Services that help run Minutes

  • Google Firebase provides authentication and the cloud database. Account details and app records are processed using those services. See Firebase's privacy and security information.
  • Google reCAPTCHA v3, through Firebase App Check, helps detect abuse. It can process device and security signals, including through cookies or similar storage, when the app loads, before you sign in. Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply to reCAPTCHA.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and can process technical request information when you visit. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

These providers operate internationally, so their processing may involve countries outside your own. Their notices describe their practices and applicable safeguards. Emailing the privacy contact also involves your email provider and Microsoft Outlook.

This policy page itself does not initialise Firebase, Google sign-in or reCAPTCHA. The app does.

Storage on your device

Minutes uses browser storage for sign-in state, preferences, cached records and the offline app. Where supported, Firestore stores pending changes on your device and sends them when the connection returns.

Signing out does not necessarily erase cached records. Removing the app or clearing browser storage does not delete cloud records, and can remove changes that have not synced. Let pending changes finish syncing before clearing storage, especially on a shared device.

Keeping, correcting and deleting information

There is no automatic expiry period for account and activity records at present. They remain until removed using the available app controls or by the administrator. The administrator also keeps migration and recovery backups; deleting a live entry or signing out does not automatically remove those copies.

To request a copy, a correction or deletion, contact the address above. Please identify the participant and the information concerned, including backup copies if relevant. The administrator may need to verify that the request is yours and review shared records and any applicable requirements. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes.

Depending on the law that applies, you may have additional rights over your personal information or the right to contact a data-protection authority. This notice does not limit those rights.

Changes to this notice

Updates will appear on this page with a new date. The notice will need reviewing if the app's audience, access controls or handling of information changes.
